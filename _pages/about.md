I am currently an Assistant Professor at the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering at the National Taiwan Normal University (NTNU). Prior to this position, I served as a Postdoctoral Researcher at National Taiwan University (NTU). Additionally, I have gained valuable experience as a Research Assistant at Academia Sinica.

My research interests encompass **computer vision**, **machine learning**, **multimedia**, and **artificial intelligence**, with a strong enthusiasm for developing innovative solutions for real-world challenges. I am dedicated to mentoring highly motivated students and fostering their growth in research. Additionally, I am open to potential collaborations and welcome opportunities to work on impactful research projects. If you are interested in collaboration (both academic and industrial), feel free to reach out.
